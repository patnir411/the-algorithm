# Personal Context for Gemini Analysis

## YOUR CURRENT SITUATION
# What's happening in your life right now? Work, relationships, stress levels, major changes?




## YOUR GOALS
# What do you want to achieve? Career, personal growth, relationships, creative projects?




## YOUR STRUGGLES
# What are you finding difficult? What patterns do you want to change?




## YOUR RELATIONSHIP WITH X/TWITTER
# How do you feel about your usage? What role does it play in your life?




## SPECIFIC QUESTIONS OR CONCERNS
# What do you want to understand better about your behavior?




## WHAT YOU'VE TRIED
# Have you tried to change your usage before? What worked or didn't work?




## YOUR VALUES
# What's most important to you? What kind of person do you want to be?




## ADDITIONAL CONTEXT
# Anything else relevant? Mental health, ADHD, work requirements, etc.

EXAMPLE:

## YOUR CURRENT SITUATION
I'm a software engineer working remotely. Often lonely during the day. Using X as my "water cooler" for social connection.

## YOUR GOALS
Want to launch my own product in the next 6 months. Need deep focus time.

## YOUR STRUGGLES
I find myself refreshing X constantly when stuck on hard problems. It's become a procrastination tool. I feel like I'm losing my ability to think deeply.

## YOUR RELATIONSHIP WITH X/TWITTER
Love the tech community and learning. Hate how it fragments my attention. Feel FOMO when I try to quit.

## SPECIFIC QUESTIONS OR CONCERNS
Why do I keep checking even when I know I have work to do? How can I use X intentionally instead of compulsively?
